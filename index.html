Akasha.ai

The operational intelligence layer for Indian enterprises.

Factories, warehouses and construction sites already own cameras, an ERP and a pile of documents. None of it talks, and nobody watches it in real time. Akasha turns that existing investment into one queryable, continuously updated picture of the business, with agents that act on it.

Eye
Edge CV on the customer's own GPU. Camera feeds in, typed events out — PPE_VIOLATION, FIRE_DETECTED, VEHICLE_ENTRY. Raw video never leaves the site.
Brain
Operational memory in one Postgres: pgvector for search, Apache AGE for the entity graph. Ask "PPE violations in Sector 4 this month, by shift" and get SQL-backed answers with citations.
Action
A LangGraph runtime wired to both: agents that trigger on camera events, read memory and send the WhatsApp, the PDF, the Tally entry.

Chef.ai

Scan your fridge. Cook something good. Share it.

Point a phone at the fridge: YOLO finds the ingredients, Gemini reads freshness and cuisine fit, and a 7-node LangGraph DAG fuses 20 pgvector candidates with 15 freshly generated recipes into a ranked top 10–12, over a 7,298-recipe corpus. ChefGPT then stays with you at the stove: a streaming WebSocket agent with 9 tools, a 4-step tool-calling loop and memory that compresses itself every 15 turns.

Artemis

On-device AI that notices trouble before you can reach for your phone.

Every 1.5 seconds, a threat engine on the phone listens and looks: Silero VAD gates a 521-class YAMNet audio classifier and 192-dim ECAPA-TDNN speaker embeddings, fused with motion, battery and network signals across 7 named risk patterns. The per-tick stream never leaves the device; one snapshot row does, only when something escalates.

The LLM agent helps, but it never decides. A deterministic rule engine gates every SOS, with a 60-second cooldown against false alarms, a 30-second dead-man's-switch heartbeat and live-trip deviation alerts. I lead a 12-person team taking it from MVP to a business.

03 Research

Doing more with less: smaller models, sparser inputs, explanations you can measure.

Under review WACV 2027 · with Dr. Gargi Srivastava

Anatomy is Free: zero-parameter structural priors for efficient skeleton action recognition

Skeleton models spend millions of parameters learning what anatomy already tells you. ShiftFuse-Zero hard-codes that structure instead, as pure tensor indexing:

  • BRASP shifts features between body regions. The left and right wrists sit 10+ hops apart in the skeleton graph; BRASP puts one beside the other in a single lookup.
  • SGPShift routes each channel to a ranked neighbour inside its body part or across a part boundary.
  • Both add no weights and no FLOPs. The GCN that follows refines a representation that is already shaped like a body.

Under review WACV 2027 · Applications track

Aligned but Inert: concept bottlenecks in dense prediction, and how to make them causal

A concept bottleneck for 3D brain-tumour segmentation (BraTS 2024) passes every test the literature uses: routing tracks the clinical concepts at 0.96 alignment. Yet forcing tokens through a different expert changes only 1.1% of voxel labels. The decoder never consults it.

The leak is a residual path through the module and skip connections around it. Blocking them fails; the optimiser compensates. Making the routing an additive term of the output works — at the cost of about one Dice point. The paper also ships a three-test diagnostic that tells consulted bottlenecks from ignored ones, with no retraining.
